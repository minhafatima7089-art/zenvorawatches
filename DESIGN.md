## Brand & Style

The brand personality marries high-horology heritage with modern electronic refinement. Targeted at discerning Pakistani collectors, technophiles, and luxury gift buyers, the interface conveys exclusivity, precision engineering, and absolute authenticity.

The aesthetic blends **Dark-Mode Minimalism** with **Tactile Luxury Accents**:
- Deep obsidian and midnight navy canvas tones eliminate glare and elevate product photography.
- Brushed champagne and metallic gold micro-accents punctuate critical actions and certifications without overpowering content.
- Clean architectural lines, razor-thin borders, and generous breathing room create an editorial, boutique-grade shopping salon.
- Localized commercial confidence is reinforced through specialized high-trust components: direct-to-concierge WhatsApp purchasing paths, authenticated serial badges, and prominent, crystal-clear Pakistani Rupee (PKR/Rs.) pricing tables.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-premium night environment, utilizing controlled luminescence and metallic accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#D4AF37` / Highlight `#E5C05B`):** Metallic Horological Gold. Used strictly for high-value affordances: primary CTA buttons, curated badges, verified authenticity seals, and active states.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A`):** Deep Slate Navy. Forms surface elevations, structured containers, filters, and product card backgrounds that float over the base.
- **Tertiary (`#25D366`):** Concierge Green (WhatsApp). Reserved exclusively for instant WhatsApp checkout actions, concierge chat triggers, and real-time stock inquiries.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0B0F17`):** Obsidian Black. The absolute foundation. Absorbs background noise and grounds high-contrast horology imagery.
- **Border & Surface Outlines (`#1E293B` to `#334155`):** Subtle slate hairline rules that carve out structure without visual weight.
- **Typography Neutrals:** High-clarity off-white (`#F8FAFC`) for primary headers and numerical pricing; cool muted silver (`#94A3B8`) for secondary specifications, movement calibers, and meta labels.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances European editorial haute-horlogerie with the clinical precision of luxury electronics.

- **Editorial Titles (`Playfair Display`):** Used for collection names, hero claims, brand narratives, and model titles. Its refined serifs communicate lineage and craftsmanship.
- **Ergonomic Body (`Manrope`):** Delivers clean readability for specifications, case dimensions, warranty conditions, and high-density checkout flows.
- **Technical Readouts (`Space Grotesk`):** Set in uppercase with wide letter spacing for reference numbers, caliber movements, water resistance ratings, and SKU IDs.
- **PKR Currency Conventions:** All prices must format the currency descriptor as `PKR` or `Rs.` using `label-tech` or medium-weight sans-serif, directly paired with bold numerals in `Manrope` (e.g., `PKR 850,000`).

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on an authentic 12-column fluid grid on desktop and tablet landscapes, collapsing down to 4 columns on mobile viewports.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, max container width `1360px`, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 3rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`.

### Rhythms & Pacing
Vertical spacing follows deliberate editorial tension:
- Dense information groupings (spec tables, serial logs) utilize tight 8px (`space-sm`) and 16px (`space-md`) increments.
- Showcase grids, featured complication sections, and gallery showcases expand across `space-xl` (40px) to 80px intervals to allow horological photography to breathe against the dark background.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on harsh drop shadows. Instead, it employs **Tonal Layering** accompanied by **Subtle Metallic Specular Highlights** and **Deep Amber/Gold Ambient Glows**.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#0B0F17` (Deep Obsidian).
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Surfaces):** `#0F172A` (Rich Slate Navy) bordered by a 1px solid `#1E293B` stroke.
- **Level 2 (Hovered & Active Cards):** `#162036` paired with a gold hairline border (`#D4AF37` at 40% opacity) and a soft ambient glow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 16px 0 rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Drawers):** `#0F172A` with 95% opacity and `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Outlined with 1px `#334155`. Casts an ambient shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.

Admin controls and utility toolbars adopt flat, low-contrast ghost borders without shadows to maintain systematic clarity.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft Geometry (`roundedness: 1`)**. 

- Base components (inputs, standard buttons, badge tags) feature crisp `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii, referencing the beveled edges of luxury watch cases and precision-cut sapphire crystals.
- Medium containers and product cards adopt `0.5rem` (8px).
- Modals, flyout panels, and sheet drawers use `0.75rem` (12px).
- Circular pills (`rounded-full`) are reserved strictly for WhatsApp interactive bubbles and numeric notification counters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Luxury Button:** Solid `#D4AF37` background, `#0B0F17` bold typography, `4px` corner radius. Hover state transitions to `#E5C05B` with subtle metallic inner glow.
- **WhatsApp Concierge Action:** `#25D366` background, crisp white typography, integrated WhatsApp SVG icon. Used for instant inquiries like "Order via WhatsApp" or "Inquire with Horologist."
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent base, 1px `#334155` border, `#F8FAFC` text. Hover shifts border to `#D4AF37` and text to `#D4AF37`.

### Product Cards
- Built on `#0F172A` with a 1px `#1E293B` border.
- Features a strict 1:1 or 4:5 aspect ratio image viewport over an obsidian neutral base.
- Badges sit top-left (e.g., `OFFICIAL WARRANTY`, `SWISS MADE`) in `label-caps` on semi-transparent dark backing.
- Details: Brand name in `label-tech`, watch model in `headline-sm`, and pricing prominently in `price-md` with `PKR` preceding the amount.
- Quick-action footer contains two distinct triggers: a compact cart icon and a dedicated WhatsApp icon button for direct purchase.

### WhatsApp Direct Badges & Chips
- Compact, high-visibility badges featuring green micro-accents.
- Informational chips: Dark slate capsule with a 1px border. Examples: `42MM`, `AUTOMATIC`, `SAPPHIRE CRYSTAL`, `IN STOCK LAHORE / KARACHI`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Background in deep `#070A10`, inset 1px border in `#1E293B`.
- Active focus state features a crisp 1px gold border (`#D4AF37`) with zero default browser outline glow.
- Placeholder text in `#64748B`. Integrated currency prefixes (e.g., "PKR") locked inside inputs with `Space Grotesk` formatting.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Custom square boxes (`4px` radius) and circular radios with dark inset backgrounds and `#334155` outlines.
- Selected state fills with `#D4AF37` and displays an obsidian black checkmark or center pip.

### Admin Controls & Data Grids
- Inventory management tables feature compact alternating rows (`#0B0F17` / `#0F172A`) separated by hairline borders.
- Stock statuses use strict semantic badges: Green for "Available", Amber for "Reserved", Red for "Sold Out".
- Quick-toggle action bars remain locked to top or bottom with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` and thin metallic delineation.
